Data Broker Tailspin Exposed Records of 40 Million People

A data broker called Tailspin left a database with the names, addresses, and phone numbers of roughly 40 million people open to the internet without a password, a breach discovered by an independent researcher.

Tailspin secured the database two days after being notified but has not said how long it was exposed or whether anyone else accessed it.

The records could be used for targeted phishing and identity fraud. Tailspin says it will notify affected people once its investigation is complete.
