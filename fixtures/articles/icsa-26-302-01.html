Fabrikam SCADA Gateway Vulnerability Actively Exploited (CVE-2026-90021)

Fabrikam has reported that a remote code execution vulnerability, CVE-2026-90021, in the SG-500 SCADA gateway was exploited before a patch was available. The gateway is deployed across water, wastewater, and energy facilities, making this a critical infrastructure concern.

Successful exploitation could allow an unauthenticated attacker to execute commands on the device and alter process data sent to operators.

Fabrikam released firmware 3.2.1 to fix the issue. CISA recommends that operators isolate the gateways from the internet and place them behind firewalls until the firmware is installed.
