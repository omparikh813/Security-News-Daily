Lakeshore Health ransomware attack disrupts hospital systems across three states

A ransomware attack on Lakeshore Health, a regional hospital network, has disrupted electronic health records and forced emergency departments in three states to divert ambulances.

The company says it detected the intrusion on Tuesday night, took systems offline, and is working with outside incident responders and law enforcement.

No ransomware gang has claimed the attack yet. Patients have been told to bring medication lists and paper records to appointments while systems are restored.
